Help: actions & links on this page
Form Teams
Opens the team formation tool to assign applicants to teams based on availability overlap.
Collect Stats
Opens a form to pull GitHub contribution stats for the session's teams.
Email Actions
  • Send application results — opens a confirmation page before emailing applicants their acceptance/rejection/waitlist results.
  • Send acceptance reminder emails — opens a confirmation page before reminding accepted applicants to confirm their spot.
  • Send team welcome emails — opens a confirmation page before emailing newly formed teams their welcome message.
Discord
  • Set up Discord — opens a confirmation page before creating the session's Discord category, team channels, and roles as a background task.
  • Tear down Discord — opens a confirmation page before archiving the session's Discord channels and roles as a background task.
  • Team messages — shows copy-paste welcome text for each team's Discord channel.
Bulk actions (the "Action:" dropdown below the list)
  • Auto-allocate Djangonauts to teams — [runs immediately] requires exactly one selected session; runs the allocation algorithm to assign eligible applicants to teams.
  • Preview rejection/waitlist/team-welcome email — sends a preview of the email to your own address only; it does not email applicants.
