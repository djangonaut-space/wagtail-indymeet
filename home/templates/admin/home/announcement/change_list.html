Help: actions & columns on this page
What happens automatically
  • Announcements still needing approval are emailed to the session's organizers roughly six days before their post date, once, via a background job.
  • Due, approved announcements (or ones that don't need approval) post to Discord on their own, via a background job.
Nothing below needs to be done for an announcement to go out on schedule — the action is only for getting ahead of that schedule.
Bulk actions (the "Action:" dropdown below the list)
  • Post selected announcements to Discord now [runs immediately] — posts the selected announcements without waiting for their post date. Announcements still awaiting approval, ones already posted, and ones whose session has no live Discord channel are skipped.
List columns
  • Needs approval — whether an organizer must approve this announcement before it can post.
  • Approved at — when an organizer approved the announcement; blank means it's still waiting (if it needs approval at all).
  • Posted at — when the announcement was posted to Discord; blank means it hasn't posted yet.
  • Emailed for approval at — when the approval-request email was sent to organizers; blank means it hasn't been sent yet.
